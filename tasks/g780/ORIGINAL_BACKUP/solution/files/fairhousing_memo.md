3 compliant, 4 carry at least one finding.

## SEC-03, SEC-07 protected-class language

Names protected-class-adjacent terms and is not the mandated disclosure:
`PROTECTED_CLASS_LANGUAGE`.

## SEC-05, SEC-07 disclaimer missing

The mandated fair-housing disclosure is not present: `DISCLAIMER_MISSING`.

## SEC-04 is the mandated-disclosure exception, not a finding

SEC-04 names protected-class terms, which looks exactly like SEC-03's violation. But
SEC-04 is itself the brokerage's mandated fair-housing disclosure, which the standard
exempts from the protected-class-language rule because the disclosure necessarily quotes
the law it is informing sellers about. The finding is `none`.
