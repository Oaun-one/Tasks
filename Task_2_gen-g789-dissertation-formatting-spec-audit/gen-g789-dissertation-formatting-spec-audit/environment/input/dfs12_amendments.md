# DFS-12 amendments

Graduate Thesis Office. Amendments to the base standard, in order of issue.

Each amendment states its own **effective date**. An amendment is in force for a
dissertation if its effective date falls on or before that dissertation's date of
submission. An amendment that is not in force has no bearing on the audit, however
recently it was published.

---

## A-1 — binding-edge margin

**Effective 2024-06-01.**

§2's minimum for the **inner** (binding-edge) margin is raised from 2.5 cm to
**2.8 cm** for every section, so that text is not lost into the gutter when the volume
is bound. The minima for the top and bottom margins are unchanged and remain the
template's own figure.

---

## A-2 — binding-edge margin, revised

**Effective 2025-01-15.**

**A-1 is superseded in full and ceases to have effect on this date.** Trial binding of
the 2024 intake showed 2.8 cm to be insufficient for volumes over 300 pages. §2's
minimum for the **inner** margin is **3.0 cm** for every section.

This figure is also the binding-edge minimum §3 uses when working out the type area a
landscape page would otherwise allow.

---

## A-3 — landscape data annexes, narrowed

**Effective 2025-09-01.**

§3's exception for landscape data annexes was being read too broadly. It applies only
where all three of the conditions listed in §3 hold, and it releases the section from
§3 alone. The binding-edge minimum in §2 is not subject to it.

---

## A-4 — leading for body sections

**Effective 2026-07-01.**

§4's required leading for sections of `section_type` `body` becomes **double**, that
is 2.0 × 12.0 pt = 24.0 pt. Front matter, back matter and annexes are unaffected and
remain at 18.0 pt.

---

## A-5 — leading recorded in millimetres

**Effective 2025-11-01.**

Leading may be recorded in millimetres as well as points. The conversion for the
purposes of this standard is **1 pt = 0.35 mm**, and the requirement is unchanged: it
is 1.5 times the body size the standard requires, irrespective of the size a section
actually uses.
